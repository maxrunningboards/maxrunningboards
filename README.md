# MAX Running Boards |  China Electric Power Running Boards Manufacturer

MAX Running Boards is a China-based manufacturer specializing in electric power running boards for trucks and SUVs. We work with automotive accessories distributors, wholesalers, 4x4 shops, and private-label brands worldwide.

Our focus is on vehicle-specific fitment, OEM and ODM manufacturing, and electric side-step solutions for global automotive markets.

## Products & Services

- Electric power running boards for trucks and SUVs
- Vehicle-specific retractable side steps
- OEM and ODM manufacturing
- Private-label branding and packaging
- Custom product development for automotive brands
